# Code of Conduct

Ognom follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The short version:

## Our standards

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to MongoDB or to open source.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and take feedback constructively.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory language, sexualised content, or publishing anyone's private information.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Enforcement

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer, [@sunilksamanta](https://github.com/sunilksamanta), privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

Depending on what happened, the response ranges from a private word to a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban from the project's spaces. The full enforcement guidelines are in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
